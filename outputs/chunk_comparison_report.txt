[TASK 4] Strategy Selection & Architectural Justification
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SELECTED STRATEGY: Hierarchical Policy-Aware Semantic Chunking (with Paragraph Fallback)

Why This Strategy Fits the Property Insurance Corpus and Retrieval Needs:

1. Complete Legal Cohesion (Eliminating Fractured Deductibles & Exceptions):
   In insurance policies (e.g., HO-3 Form), every coverage term is governed by
   specific perils, exceptions, and deductibles (e.g., HO-04-95 adds water backup
   up to $10,000 subject to a $1,000 special deductible). If a fixed window cuts
   between the peril and the deductible, the adjuster assistant will retrieve the
   coverage limit while missing the required deductible—leading directly to bad-faith misquotes.

2. Section Breadcrumb Preservation for Grounded Citations:
   Our assistant prompt requires explicit citation of section numbers and policy forms.
   Hierarchical chunking prepends '[SECTION I – PROPERTY COVERAGES > COVERAGE C]' directly
   into the chunk metadata and content, giving the vector database and LLM unambiguous
   hierarchy context.

3. Optimal Retrieval Window vs. Context Budget:
   At an average of ~380-420 tokens per chunk, retrieving top-5 chunks consumes ~2,000
   tokens. On gpt-4o-mini ($0.15 / 1M input tokens), the input prompt cost is less than
   $0.0003 per query, while comfortably remaining far below model context limits.

4. Overlap Efficiency:
   Paragraph-level overlap ensures continuity between adjacent provisions without
   inflating indexing storage or embedding costs needlessly (1.18x - 1.25x token overhead).
