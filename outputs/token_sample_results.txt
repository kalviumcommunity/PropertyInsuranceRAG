Key Takeaways:
 1. English prose averages ~4 characters per token (~1.2 - 1.3 tokens per word).
 2. Long compound words and legal jargon (e.g. 'Wohngebäudeversicherungsgesellschaft')
    split into multiple subwords, causing high tokens-per-word ratios.
 3. Code, JSON syntax, and alphanumeric policy IDs split into frequent single-character
    or punctuation tokens, increasing token density.
 4. Non-Latin scripts (Hindi, Japanese) require multiple tokens per character/word because
    standard byte-pair encodings (cl100k_base) allocate fewer dedicated vocab tokens to them.
